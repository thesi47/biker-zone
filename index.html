Frequently Asked Questions

What are the most important things I should know about riding a bike?
Safety First! Always obey the rules of the road. Obey all traffic signals, signs, and laws. Get in the mindset of “driving” your bike—not just “riding” your bike. This will help you be a more focused and legally compliant bike rider.
How can I tell if my helmet is old and I need a new one?
Protect Your Head First! If your helmet shows cracks, loose padding, worn straps, or has been involved in a crash, it's time to replace it. Helmets weaken over time, even without visible damage, so staying updated ensures maximum protection and safer rides.
My bike has been in storage is it safe to ride?
Check Before You Roll! A bike that's been unused for a long time may have hidden issues. Inspect the tires, brakes, and chain carefully before riding. Look for rust, low pressure, or stiff movement. Make sure everything works smoothly so you stay safe and in control on the road.
What rules should I follow when riding my bike?
Ride Smart, Ride Safe! Always follow the rules of the road and respect traffic signals, signs, and lane markings. Use proper hand signals when turning or stopping and stay in your designated bike lane whenever possible. Stay alert, avoid distractions, and make sure other road users can clearly see you at all times.
